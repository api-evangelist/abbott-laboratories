# Lingo E-Commerce Site

> Direct-to-consumer e-commerce platform for Lingo products

## About Lingo

Lingo is a continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) system designed to help users track glucose levels and understand their metabolic health.

## Subscription Service

- Subscription products available for automatic recurring delivery
- Manage subscription settings through account dashboard at /account/subscription
- Update payment method, shipping address, or cancel anytime

## Shipping & Returns

- Track orders in real-time through account dashboard
- Return requests can be initiated at /orders/returns/[orderId]
- International shipping available to UK
